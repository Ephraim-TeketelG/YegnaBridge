Our Core Philosophy

Leadership begins with listening.

The word Yegna means ours in Amharic, and Bridge signifies crossing challenges together. We believe the biggest gap in supporting vulnerable families is not just physical supplies, but the lack of someone willing to sit down and listen to real stories.

When we assist a mother or a child, our goal is never to hand out temporary items for praise. Our aim is to build family, restore dignity, and walk side by side through life.

Not charity. Family.

Shinshicho Branch

Empowering a Mother

We met a mother who was struggling to provide for her daughter after her husband was killed over a land dispute. She didn't want permanent charity; she wanted to work.

With the support of our donors, we raised approximately 10,000 ETB to purchase a heavy-duty plastic market shelter, potatoes, peas, salt, and coffee. We recently visited her again and found that she had successfully retailed her stock twice. She is now standing on her own feet—the ultimate goal of YegnaBridge.

Addis Ababa Branch

Easter Meal Sharing

We celebrated the launch of our Addis Ababa branch by organizing a meal sharing program during the Easter season. We reached out to families and individuals, sharing not just food and clothing, but culture, warmth, and belonging.

It was a day filled with Ethiopian traditions, showing that true support is holistic—it nourishes the body and the soul.

Shinshicho Branch

Meskerem 1 Meal Share

At YegnaBridge, we believe that small acts of compassion can create ripples of change. On Meskerem 1, we organized a massive meal-sharing program with the community.

With the support of our team, we reached out to over 10 individuals and families. A shared meal means warmth, dignity, and a reminder that no one is alone. We aren't just distributing food; we are building bridges of care, one meal and one smile at a time.
