Our Core Philosophy

Leadership begins with listening.

The word Yegna means ours in Amharic, and Bridge signifies crossing challenges together. We believe the biggest gap in supporting vulnerable families is not just physical supplies, but the lack of someone willing to sit down and listen to real stories.

When we assist a mother or a child, our goal is never to hand out temporary items for praise. Our aim is to build family, restore dignity, and walk side by side through life.

Not charity. Family.
