Our Core Philosophy

Leadership begins with listening.

The word Yegna means ours in Amharic, and Bridge signifies crossing challenges together. We believe the biggest gap in supporting vulnerable families is not just physical supplies, but the lack of someone willing to sit down and listen to real stories.

When we assist a mother or a child, our goal is never to hand out temporary items for praise. Our aim is to build family, restore dignity, and walk side by side through life.

Not charity. Family.

Shinshicho Branch Initiative

Turning a Dream Into Reality

In our early outreaches in Shinshicho, we met a mother who had faced unimaginable hardship after her husband was killed over a land dispute, leaving her to raise her young son alone with no home or income.

When we asked her what she needed most, she did not ask for handouts. She said she wanted to work. She wanted a sustainable way to earn a living.

With generous support from donors and community members, we raised funds to help her launch a marketplace business. We purchased a heavy-duty plastic sheet for her market shelter, peas, salt, coffee, potatoes, and cooking oil.

Recently, she shared that she bought more stock in bulk twice and successfully retailed it all. Seeing her running her own business independently is the true mission of YegnaBridge in action.

Addis Ababa Branch Milestone

The Easter Outreach Program

Today marked an important step for our Addis Ababa branch as we completed our first major outreach activity. We identified and supported a single mother raising four children under difficult conditions.

Through the collective effort of our members and supporters, we provided essential supplies valued between 14,000 and 15,000 ETB, including flour, cooking oil, macaroni, rice, onions, salt, soap, and sanitary pads, alongside clothing contributions and cash support.

Beyond material assistance, our team spent quality time listening to her story and sharing moments of connection, laughter, and traditional Ethiopian culture.

Central Team & Structure

Building Our Foundation Together

The YegnaBridge central team holds regular meetings to align our mission across branches in Shinshicho and Addis Ababa. Our work is driven by core values:

  • Compassion: Serving with heart and deep empathy.
  • Share Love: Because family is built on mutual care.
  • Dignity: Ensuring every person is respected and seen.
  • Empowerment: Lifting lives so individuals can stand strong independently.

We believe leadership begins with listening, and our team remains dedicated to walking side by side with those we serve.
