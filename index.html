Leadership begins
with listening.

The word Yegna means ours in Amharic, and Bridge signifies crossing challenges together. We believe the biggest gap in supporting vulnerable communities is not just physical supplies, but the profound lack of someone willing to sit down and listen.

When we assist a mother or a child in Shinshicho or Addis Ababa, our goal is never to hand out temporary items for praise. Our mandate is to restore dignity and walk side-by-side through life.

Shinshicho Branch

Empowering a Mother

We met a mother who was struggling to provide for her daughter after her husband was killed over a land dispute. She did not want permanent charity; she wanted to work.

With the support of our donors, we raised approximately 10,000 ETB to purchase a heavy-duty plastic market shelter, potatoes, peas, salt, and coffee. She has now successfully retailed her stock twice and is standing on her own feet.

Addis Ababa Branch

Easter Meal Sharing

We celebrated the launch of our Addis Ababa branch by organizing a meal sharing program during the Easter season. We reached out to families and individuals, sharing not just food and clothing, but culture, warmth, and belonging.

It was a day filled with Ethiopian traditions, proving that true support nourishes both the body and the soul.

Community Initiative

Meskerem 1 Meal Share

On Meskerem 1, we organized a massive meal-sharing program, reaching out to over 10 individuals and families.

A shared meal means dignity. It is a reminder that no one is walking through their struggles alone. We aren't just distributing food; we are building bridges of care, one smile at a time.
