Moments of Connection

Outreach Gallery

A chronological view of our milestones, from our very first step to establishing branches and transforming lives.

01

Our First Step

Our foundational outreach in Shinshicho where we first connected deeply with families, marking the true beginning of the YegnaBridge mission.

02

Expanding to Addis Ababa

The official campaign poster calling for dedicated youth volunteers to help us build our second branch in the capital city.

03

Growing the Family

The mobilization and registration of our new Addis Ababa volunteer team, uniting dedicated youth ready to serve the community.

04

The 15,000 ETB Campaign

Our unified fundraising effort to gather essential resources for vulnerable families and street children in need of immediate support.

05

The Kindness Challenge

Engaging our youth and local community through active moments of compassion, shared love, and small acts that make a huge difference.

06

Easter Meal Sharing

The milestone launch of our Addis Ababa branch, sharing food, clothing, and cultural connection with struggling families during the holiday.

07

Empowering a Mother

Moving beyond temporary aid by providing capital and a market shelter to help a single mother in Shinshicho achieve financial independence.
