Leadership begins
with listening.

The word Yegna means ours in Amharic. We believe the biggest gap in supporting vulnerable families is not physical supplies, but the lack of someone willing to sit down and listen to real stories. Our goal is never to hand out temporary items for praise. We restore dignity and walk side by side through life.

Shinshicho Branch

Empowering a Mother

We met a mother who was struggling to provide for her daughter after her husband was killed over a land dispute. She did not want permanent charity; she wanted to work.

With the support of our donors, we raised approximately 10,000 ETB to purchase a heavy-duty plastic market shelter, potatoes, peas, salt, and coffee. She has now successfully retailed her stock twice and is standing on her own feet. This is the ultimate goal of YegnaBridge.

Addis Ababa Branch

Easter Meal Sharing

We celebrated the launch of our Addis Ababa branch by organizing a meal sharing program during the Easter season. We reached out to families and individuals, sharing not just food and clothing, but culture, warmth, and belonging.

It was a day filled with Ethiopian traditions, showing that true support is holistic—it nourishes both the body and the soul.

Community Initiative

Meskerem 1 Meal Share

At YegnaBridge, we believe that small acts of compassion can create ripples of change. On Meskerem 1, we organized a massive meal-sharing program with the community.

With the support of our team, we reached out to over 10 individuals and families. A shared meal means warmth, dignity, and a reminder that no one is alone. We aren't just distributing food; we are building bridges of care, one meal and one smile at a time.
